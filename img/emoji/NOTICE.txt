Emoji: Google Noto Emoji (Apache 2.0).
The emoji pictures in this folder are from Google's Noto Emoji project, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji
(main branch, 2D/svg/emoji_u<codepoint>.svg), licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0 (LICENSE.txt).
The four flag pictures (1f1ff_1f1e6 ZA, 1f1ec_1f1e7 GB, 1f1ea_1f1fa EU, 1f1fa_1f1f8 US) are from the same repository's
third_party/region-flags/svg, which are in the public domain or otherwise exempt from copyright.
Changes made: XML declaration, comments, DOCTYPE/ENTITY declarations and Adobe metadata removed (entity references inlined),
whitespace between tags removed, files renamed to <codepoint>.svg, a viewBox added to the EU flag. Drawings unchanged.
Downloaded 12 Nov 2026 for AgriInsights (SA -513 / UK -432).
