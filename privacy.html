Who is responsible for your information

AgriInsights is run by AgriInsights Technologies Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17458627), 44a Upcerne Road, London, SW10 0SQ. We are the controller of the information described here, except your workers’ information (see below).

For anything about your information, contact Jeevan Athwal at jeevan.athwal@agriinsightstech.com, or write to us at the address above.

What we collect

  • About you: your name, email address, farm name, country and region, farm size and the tax details you enter.
  • Your farm records: income, expenses, crops, livestock (including ear tags and movement records), orchards, assets, loans and the documents you upload.
  • About your workers: if you use the Workers section, their names, pay, National Insurance details, contracts, leave and deductions.
  • Where your farm is: if you use Rainfall on the computer, the postcode or place you choose and its coordinates. That is the place you pick, not your device’s location.
  • On the phone app, only when you allow it:
    • Location: when you record diesel or a rain reading, the phone can note where it was taken, if you have allowed location for AgriInsights. It is read only at that moment, never in the background, and you can switch it off in your phone’s settings; the app works without it.
    • Photos: a slip, invoice or certificate you photograph or choose is stored with the record it belongs to. The figures on a slip are read on the phone itself; the photo is not sent anywhere to be read.
    • Your phone: a record of each phone paired with your farm (its name and when it was paired), so you can see and remove it.
  • Technical: sign-in records and basic device information needed to keep your account secure.

What we use it for, and the lawful basis for each

  • Running your account and the service: your profit, cash flow, budgets, tax estimates, reports and compliance records, kept in step across your computer and your phones. Basis: our contract with you.
  • Keeping the service secure and fixing faults. Basis: our legitimate interest in a working, secure service.
  • Answering a legal request from HMRC or another authority, only where the law requires it. Basis: legal obligation.
  • News and offers by email: only if you tick the separate box for it. Basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time in Settings or from the link in any of those emails.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your farm data for advertising. We make no decisions about you by automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Your workers’ information

Where you record worker details, you are the controller under UK GDPR and we are your processor: we process that information only on your instruction, and only to run the service for you. You are responsible for telling your workers what you record and why, and for having a lawful basis to record it. We can generate a worker notice to help you do this.

Who we share it with

Only with the services needed to run AgriInsights, under contract, who may not use it for their own purposes:

  • our hosting and database provider, Supabase (servers in London), which also sends our sign-in and account emails;
  • if you use Rainfall, Open-Meteo (Switzerland), which receives only your farm’s coordinates in order to return rainfall for that spot, and postcodes.io (UK), which receives only a postcode to find where it is. No name, no farm records and nothing about you goes with either.

We share information with HMRC or another authority only where the law requires it, or where you choose to export or send a report yourself.

Where it is stored, and transfers outside the UK

Your data is stored in our hosted database with Supabase, on servers in London. Supabase is a US company, so its staff may reach the data from outside the UK to run and support the service; we rely on the safeguards UK law requires for that, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum in Supabase’s data processing agreement. Open-Meteo is in Switzerland, which the UK recognises as giving adequate protection.

How long we keep it

While your account is open we keep your farm records so the app can work for you. When you close it, you can download everything first; we then delete your farm and its records within 30 days, and keep only our own billing records.

Keeping your farm’s records for as long as the law asks is your duty as the farmer, not ours. Keep your own copy for at least:

Business records (sole trader or partnership)5 years after the 31 January filing deadline
Business records (limited company)6 years from the end of the company’s year
VAT records6 years
Payroll (PAYE) records3 years after the end of the tax year
Minimum wage records6 years
Right-to-work copies2 years after the person stops working for you

How we protect it

Your data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted to your own farm by database-level security rules, so no other farm can read your records. A phone joins your farm only with a short-lived pairing code made on your computer, and you can remove a phone at any time. Access to production systems is limited to authorised personnel.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, and to correct it, delete it, restrict it, move it to another service or object to how we use it. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time. To use any of these rights, contact jeevan.athwal@agriinsightstech.com. We answer within one month.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Giving us your information

Your farm details are needed to run the service under our contract with you: without them the app cannot work out your figures or produce your reports. The marketing box is optional, and so are location and photos on the phone.
